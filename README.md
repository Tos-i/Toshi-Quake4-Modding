All weapons have been modified to at least a small degree to match certain weapon archetypes in Destiny:
- blaster now has a slower fire rate, no charge mode, and fires mini rockets
- machine gun has a slightly smaller magazine and slower fire rate
- shotgun has a slightly smaller mag tube, more pellets per shot, and faster fire rate
- hyperblaster now fires at a much slower rate, but now fires multiple bolts at once; basically a slower but more punchier shotgun
- grenade launcher now fires much faster and has grenades explode on impact with anything, but with a smaller drum
- rocket launcher now has a 3 round tube, and reloads near instantly after not firing for a moment
- nailgun fires slightly faster
- railgun has adopted the blaster's charge function to fire, being able to let off less powerful shots when not fully charged
- lightning gun expends ammo much faster and deals more damage
